The goal of Cyan Plex's rules is to provide a secure and welcoming environment for all users across all platforms. The Moderation Team retains the authority to impose sanctions as they see fit and to extend forgiveness on an individual basis. This article serves only as a general guideline — you accept these guidelines by using our platforms.

### ✉️ Chat Rules

1. Keep the chat clean and friendly.
2. Be respectful to other members.
3. No spamming or flooding the chat.
4. No NSFW content or discussions.
5. No harassment, bullying, or hate speech. Use appropriate channels for discussions.
6. No advertising without permission.
7. Follow Discord's Terms of Service.
8. Respect the moderators' decisions. Keep discussions on-topic.
9. Have fun and enjoy the community!

### 🕹️ Gameplay Rules

1. Do not use cheats to gain an unfair advantage.
2. Keep the server clean, friendly, and balanced.
3. Do not build any kind of farm or build that can harm the server.
4. Maintain chat rules to help us as well as yourself.
5. Do not beg for rank, role, or items.
6. Real money trading is not allowed.
7. Use your common sense!

<Warning>
  Breaking these rules may result in a mute, kick, or ban at the Moderation Team's discretion.
</Warning>
