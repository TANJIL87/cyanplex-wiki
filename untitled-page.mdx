---
title: "Clan"
---

Clans let you team up with other players, build your own group, and play together.

<Tip>
  **Good news:** almost everything can be done from the **Clan GUI**.
</Tip>

Open it anytime with:

```text
/clan
```

You can use the buttons in the menu instead of typing commands.

---

## 1. Creating a Clan

Want to make your own clan?

### Step 1

Open the Clan GUI:

```text
/clan
```

### Step 2

Click **Create Clan**.

### Step 3

Enter your clan name and tag.

That's it! You are now the **Clan Leader**.

You can start inviting your friends.

---

## 2. Joining a Clan

Someone invited you to their clan?

### Step 1

Open:

```text
/clan
```

### Step 2

Find the clan invitation.

### Step 3

Click **Accept**.

You are now a clan member.

You can also join using:

```text
/clan join <clan>
```

---

## 3. Inviting Players

Want your friends to join?

### Step 1

Open:

```text
/clan
```

### Step 2

Open **Members**.

### Step 3

Click **Invite Player**.

### Step 4

Enter their username.

The player will receive an invitation.

> You need the right clan permission to invite players.

---

## 4. Clan Management

If you are a Clan Leader or have management permissions, you can manage your clan from the Clan GUI.

Open:

```text
/clan
```

You can manage:

- Members
- Clan roles
- Clan settings
- Clan homes
- Allies
- Rivals
- Clan description
- Clan tag
- Clan chat
- More clan options

---

## 5. Managing Members

Open:

**Clan → Members**

From here you can see everyone in your clan.

Depending on your permissions, you can:

- Invite players
- Remove players
- Promote members
- Demote members
- Manage their clan role

### Remove a Player

Select the player and click **Kick**.

### Promote a Player

Select the player and choose a higher role.

### Demote a Player

Select the player and choose a lower role.

---

## 6. Clan Roles

Roles decide what your members can do.

Open:

**Clan → Roles**

You can give members different roles, such as:

- Member
- Moderator
- Leader

Each role can have different permissions.

For example, you can allow your moderators to invite players without giving them full leader permissions.

---

## 7. Clan Settings

Open:

**Clan → Settings**

This is where you control how your clan works.

You can find options for things like:

- Who can invite players
- Who can manage members
- Clan visibility
- Friendly fire
- Clan chat
- Other clan features

Click a setting to change it.

> Some settings can only be changed by the Clan Leader or players with the correct permission.

---

## 8. Clan Chat

Want to talk only with your clan?

Use:

```text
/clan chat
```

This switches your chat to **Clan Chat**.

Use the command again to return to normal chat.

You can also access Clan Chat from the Clan GUI.

---

## 9. Friendly Fire

Friendly Fire controls whether clan members can damage each other.

Open:

**Clan → Settings → Friendly Fire**

Turn it **ON** if clan members should be able to fight each other.

Turn it **OFF** if they should not.

---

## 10. Clan Homes

Your clan can save locations as **Clan Homes**.

Open:

**Clan → Homes**

From here you can:

- Create a home
- Teleport to a home
- Delete a home
- View your clan homes

You can also use:

```text
/clan home list
```

---

## 11. Allies

Want another clan to be your friend?

Open:

**Clan → Allies**

You can:

- Send an alliance request
- Accept an alliance
- Decline an alliance
- Remove an alliance
- View your allied clans

Allies are useful when playing together with another clan.

---

## 12. Rivals

Want to mark another clan as a rival?

Open:

**Clan → Rivals**

You can:

- Add a rival
- Accept a rival request
- Decline a rival request
- Remove a rival
- View your rivals

---

## 13. Clan Info

Want to see your clan's information?

Open:

```text
/clan
```

Then select **Clan Info**.

You can see things like:

- Clan name
- Clan tag
- Leader
- Members
- Clan level
- Allies
- Rivals
- Other clan information

You can also use:

```text
/clan info
```

---

## 14. Clan Description

Your clan can have its own description.

Open:

**Clan → Settings → Description**

Write something short about your clan.

For example:

> The strongest builders in Cyan Plex!

---

## 15. Clan Tag

Your clan tag is the short name shown for your clan.

Example:

**WOLVES**

You can change it from the Clan GUI if you have permission.

---

## 16. Clan Level

Your clan can progress and unlock more features.

Open:

**Clan → Level**

Here you can see:

- Your current level
- Your progress
- What you need for the next level
- Available upgrades

---

## 17. Clan Rewards

Some clan progress can give you rewards.

Open:

**Clan → Rewards**

If you have a reward available, simply click it to claim it.

---

## 18. Clan Activity

Want to see what happened in your clan?

Open:

**Clan → Activity**

You may see things like:

- Players joining
- Players leaving
- Promotions
- Demotions
- Invitations
- Other clan actions

---

# Useful Commands

You don't need to remember these because the **Clan GUI does almost everything**, but these commands can be useful.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/clan` | Open the Clan GUI |
| `/clan create <tag>` | Create a clan |
| `/clan join <clan>` | Join a clan |
| `/clan leave` | Leave your clan |
| `/clan info` | View clan information |
| `/clan list` | View clans |
| `/clan chat` | Toggle clan chat |
| `/clan ff` | Toggle friendly fire |

---

# Leaving a Clan

Want to leave your current clan?

Open:

```text
/clan
```

Go to **Settings** or **Clan Options** and choose **Leave Clan**.

You can also use:

```text
/clan leave
```

> Clan Leaders cannot leave normally. Transfer the leadership first.

---

# Disbanding a Clan

If you are the Clan Leader and want to completely delete your clan, use:

```text
/clan disband
```

**Be careful!** Disbanding your clan removes it completely.

---

# That's It!

You don't need to memorize all the commands.

Just remember:

```text
/clan
```

**Open the GUI → find what you need → click the button.**

Most clan features are right there.
