The Forge in Cyan Plex is a custom workstation designed to emulate the tools and ambiance of a traditional blacksmith's forge. It's used to craft and repair tools, weapons, and armor, providing players with a specialized crafting environment.

![The Forge Craft Station](https://cyan-plex.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3633916600-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FOOvmJ4P9YJ9Bn06TK1JX%252Fuploads%252FQlxCK8stDwRaAtQ0074l%252Fthe-forge.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D6acdfca9-49e7-46c4-9408-d17026ccd3ea&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=c0c704c699664bddc156dd956400f129&sv=3)
