## Clint the Blacksmith

Clint is a highly skilled and intelligent blacksmith, renowned for his ability to craft exceptional armor and tools. With a keen understanding of metallurgy and a meticulous approach to his craft, he can transform raw materials into powerful and aesthetically pleasing items. However, Clint requires both time and specific prerequisites from the adventurer seeking his services.

## Crafting Process

To acquire one of Clint's masterpieces, adventurers must provide the specified materials and adhere to the outlined crafting time. The **Mining Level XXX** requirement ensures that only those skilled enough in mining can commission these items.

<Note>
  The **Omni Blueprint** is a rare and crucial component — it is indispensable for the creation of these extraordinary pieces.
</Note>

Clint's craftsmanship guarantees not only formidable protection but also an aesthetic appeal that reflects the expertise of a true artisan. Adventurers seeking his services should approach him well-prepared, ensuring they possess the necessary materials and meet the required skill level to unlock the full potential of his creations.
