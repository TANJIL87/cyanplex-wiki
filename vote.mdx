Cyan Plex has a voting system where you can vote for the server and get rewards in return. Voting daily gives you awesome in-game rewards and helps us attract more players!

<Note>
  Open the Vote GUI in-game at any time with `/vote`.
</Note>

After voting on all the voting sites, you will be rewarded in-game. Remember to be **online** when you vote to receive your rewards.

## Vote Rewards

Each vote across all three sites earns you:

- 🗝️ **3x Vote Key**
- 💰 **৳500** in-game money
- 🏡 **50 Claim Blocks**

## Vote Links

Click each link below and enter your in-game username to vote:

1. [minecraft-mp.com](https://minecraft-mp.com/server/292677/vote/)
2. [minecraft-server.net](https://minecraft-server.net/vote/cyan_plex/)
3. [topminecraftservers.org](https://topminecraftservers.org/vote/24268)

<Warning>
  Make sure to enter your username **exactly** as it appears in-game. A typo means you won't receive your rewards.
</Warning>

</Tip>
