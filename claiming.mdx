Claiming is a way to protect all of your items from other players. This will allow you to create a safe place for your chests and builds — no one will be able to open your chests or break blocks without your permission.

## Steps to Create a Claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark your corners">
    Hold a **wooden shovel** in your hand and right-click one corner of your land, then right-click the opposite corner. A claim will be created automatically, and gold blocks with glowstone will appear to mark the boundary.

    ![Claim creation](https://cyan-plex.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3633916600-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FOOvmJ4P9YJ9Bn06TK1JX%252Fuploads%252FfC6l5jVZVumtrV9Y9skb%252Fphoto_6338863153684789890_y.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dd4e93a45-2c67-48c9-b943-a77c588aee47&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=f0f2db49c276da7f781f12307845e8b0&sv=3)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand your claim">
    To extend your claim, right-click the glowstone at a corner and drag it to the block you want to include.

    ![Extending a claim](https://cyan-plex.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3633916600-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FOOvmJ4P9YJ9Bn06TK1JX%252Fuploads%252Fjr689gvsvj8QbEwtEDqL%252Fphoto_6338863153684789889_y.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D312b288a-9e94-4d95-86d0-78158663bdde&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=1d8ea992def37db44c4f4d03b4fb0e1d&sv=3)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Useful Commands

| Command | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| `/AbandonClaim` | Deletes the claim you're standing in. |
| `/ClaimExplosions` | Toggles if explosions are allowed in the claim. |
| `/Trust <user>` | Gives another player permission to edit in your claim. |
| `/UnTrust <user>` | Revokes any permissions granted to a player in your claim. |
| `/AccessTrust` | Gives a player permission to use your buttons, levers, and beds. |
| `/ContainerTrust` | Gives a player permission to use your buttons, levers, beds, crafting gear, containers, and animals. |
| `/TrustList` | Lists the permissions for the claim you're standing in. |
| `/PermissionTrust` | Grants a player permission to share their permission level with others. |
| `/Untrust All` | Removes all permissions for all players in your claim. |
| `/AbandonAllClaims` | Deletes all of your claims. |
| `/ClaimsList` | Lists a player's claims and claim block details. |
| `/Trapped` | Gets a player out of a land claim they're trapped inside. |
