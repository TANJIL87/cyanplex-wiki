Pebbles are Cyan Plex's premium currency, used to buy special items from the Pebble Merchant. To access the Pebble Shop, type `/warp pebble` — you'll be teleported right next to the Merchant.

![Pebble Merchant](https://cyan-plex.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3633916600-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FOOvmJ4P9YJ9Bn06TK1JX%252Fuploads%252FPMuilmhdDETwKSZvVkbz%252FUntitled184_20240325114441-min.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D743ef1cc-ba95-463c-b051-897a67dd5c58&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=5b8e3eb62f19c8a0cb7960a68c6244a9&sv=3)

Clicking on the NPC opens the Pebble Shop GUI. From there you can buy:

- 🗝️ **Crate Keys** — Epic Keys, Mythic Keys, Monthly Keys, and more
- ✈️ **Fly Time**
- 🏡 **Claim Blocks**
- 🏷️ **Tags** and other cosmetics

Hover over any item in the shop to see exactly what it costs and what it does.

## How to Get Pebbles

Pebble is a premium currency. You can purchase them directly on the store at [store.cyanplex.net](https://store.cyanplex.net) using real money to support the server.

You can also earn Pebbles **for free** through:

- 🎣 Fishing contests
- 🗳️ Voting for the server
- 📋 Completing quests and challenges
- 🎁 Giveaways

<Tip>
  Voting daily is one of the best free ways to stack up Pebbles over time. Check the [Vote](/vote) page to get started!
</Tip>
