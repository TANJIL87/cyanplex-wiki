Your fishing stats give you a full overview of your progress. Open them with `/fish stats`, or from the [Fish Menu](/fishing/fish-menu) by clicking on your head/avatar.

![Fish Stats](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FyFNagmtp73qWr3SQxbuS%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

## What You Can See

- **Left green book** — Your fishing numbers: total fish caught, types collected, and other catch statistics.
- **Fishing rod (right)** — Your current fishing level, unused skill points available to spend, and your current **entropy** balance.

## What is Entropy?

Entropy is the fishing currency used to craft and apply [augments](/fishing/fishing-augments) to your fishing rod. You earn entropy by catching fish and gutting them at the Weighing Station.
