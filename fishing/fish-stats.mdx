## What You Can See

- **Left green book** — Your fishing numbers: total fish caught, types collected, and other catch statistics.
- **Fishing rod (right)** — Your current fishing level, unused skill points available to spend, and your current **entropy** balance.

## What is Entropy?

Entropy is the fishing currency used to craft and apply [augments](/fishing/fishing-augments) to your fishing rod. You earn entropy by catching fish and gutting them at the Weighing Station.
