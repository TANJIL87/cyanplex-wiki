Fishing Deliveries (`/fish deliveries`) let you turn your caught fish into bigger entropy rewards by completing timed delivery orders. It's one of the best ways to stock up on [augment](/fishing/fishing-augments) materials.

<Note>
  Before diving into deliveries, make sure you're familiar with [Fishing Augments](/fishing/fishing-augments) — many delivery rewards are tied to augment materials.
</Note>

## How Deliveries Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Earn a delivery slip">
    For every **200 fish you catch** (base level), a new delivery slip (piece of paper) appears in your deliveries menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the delivery details">
    Hover over the paper to see:

    - The delivery's **star rating** (1-3 stars)
    - The **fish required** to complete it
    - The **time** it will take to deliver
    - The **reward** you'll receive
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather the fish and submit">
    Put the required fish in your inventory, then go back to the delivery menu and click the paper. It will turn into an **empty minecart**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your reward">
    Once the delivery time has passed, open the fishing menu and click the **chest** to redeem. Entropy rewards are added to your stats automatically; other prizes go to your inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Possible Rewards

- Entropy crystals (each redeems for 10K entropy)
- Crab Claws, Crab Scales, Squid Tentacles, Dolphin Tails
- Vouchers to boost entropy, lure more crabs, lure more Platinum or Mythical fish, and more

## Delivery Menu Upgrades

The bottom row of the deliveries menu offers purchasable upgrades (paid in entropy):

| Upgrade Item | Name | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minecart with Chest | **Increase Capacity** | Increases the number of deliveries you can hold |
| Clock | **Overclocked** | Reduces the time it takes to complete a delivery |
| Bucket of Pufferfish | **Expert Deliverer** | Reduces the amount of fish needed for a delivery |
| Emerald | **Pay Rise** | Earn more entropy per completed delivery |
| Gold Nugget | **Lucky Charm** | Reduces the number of fish required to earn a new delivery (starts at 200 base fish) |
