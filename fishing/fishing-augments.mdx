Fishing augments are like enchantments for your fishing rod, powered by **entropy** (the fishing currency). Use `/fish augment` to open the augments menu, or `/fish augments` to see your current augments list.

![Augments Menu](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FiVJd684dWskukxeTxEyM%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

To see what each augment does, click on the **book & quill** at the top of the menu and hover over the augment balls.

## How to Apply an Augment

1. Gather all the required crafting materials for the augment.
2. Drop all the materials into a **water-filled cauldron**.
3. Right-click the cauldron with your **fishing rod** — the augment ball will appear.
4. Reopen the augments menu and place:
   - Your **fishing rod** under the first book
   - The **augment ball** under the second book
   - Then click the **green box** under the third book

![Augment Application](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FRblE1abHJOt3DnklkUyU%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

<Tip>
  You can withdraw entropy by clicking the paper in the bottom left — it comes out as **10K entropy nuggets** (enchanted iron nuggets). Right-click a nugget to use it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You can remove all augments from your rod for **25K entropy**. All augment balls will be returned to your inventory.
</Note>
