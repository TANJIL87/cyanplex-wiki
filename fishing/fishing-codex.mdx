Open the Fishing Codex with `/fish codex`. Think of it as a **Pokédex for fishing** — it tracks all the fish types you've discovered and how many of each you've caught.

## Fish Tiers

There are 6 tiers of fish, each with a different sell price at `/fish shop`:

| Tier | Shop Value | Variations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥉 **Bronze** | \$100 | 34 |
| 🥈 **Silver** | \$550 | 21 |
| 🥇 **Gold** | \$1,500 | 18 |
| 💎 **Diamond** | \$5,000 | 15 |
| 🏆 **Platinum** | \$10,000 | 23 |
| ⚗️ **Mythical** | \$25,000 | 14 |

## What to Do With Your Fish

Once you've caught fish, you have four options:

- **Sell** them at [/fish shop](/fishing/fish-shop) for instant money
- **Gut** them for entropy (fishing currency)
- **Weigh** them at the [Weighing Station](/fishing/the-weighing-station) to gamble for a higher price
- **Save** them to fulfil [Fishing Deliveries](/fishing/fishing-deliveries) for entropy rewards
