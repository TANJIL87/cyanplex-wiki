The Weighing Station (`/fish scales`) has two functions: **gambling for higher sell prices** or **gutting fish for entropy**. It's a key stop if you want to maximise earnings or stock up on fishing currency.

![Weighing Station](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252F7sM2D2s8h1EAK0E2ZuAI%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

## Weighing Fish (Gambling for Better Prices)

Add your fish to the menu, then choose your **risk level** on the right side:

| Block | Risk Level |
| --- | --- |
| Coal Block | Lowest risk |
| Iron Block | Low risk |
| Gold Block | Medium risk |
| Diamond Block | Highest risk |

Once you've chosen, click the **bottom anvil** to confirm. Your fish will be assigned new prices — different from the default [Fishing Codex](/fishing/fishing-codex) values.

<Tip>
  The **Trophy** augment increases your chance to profit at the Weighing Station!
</Tip>

## Gutting Fish (for Entropy)

Gutting fish earns you **entropy** (fishing currency) instead of in-game money. This is useful when you need entropy to craft [augments](/fishing/fishing-augments). Once you gut fish, they leave your inventory and entropy is added to your balance.

### Entropy Per Tier

| Tier | Entropy Gained |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | 40 |
| Silver | 120 |
| Gold | 200 |
| Diamond | 500 |
| Platinum | 1,000 |

<Note>
  Mythical fish gut values are not listed — weigh your options before gutting your rarest catches!
</Note>
