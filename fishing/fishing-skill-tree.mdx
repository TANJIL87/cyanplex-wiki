Open your skill tree with `/fish skills`. You earn **1 skill point per fishing level** and can spend those points to permanently improve your fishing in various ways.

![Fishing Skill Tree](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FQXnrcprLNjqP2iEiIix4%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

There are two tiers of skills:

- **Top row** — Cost **1 point** each to upgrade; can have multiple points applied to amplify the effect.
- **Bottom row** — Cost **15 points** each to unlock; powerful one-time upgrades.

<Tip>
  Not happy with how you spent your points? Pay **50K entropy** to reset all points and reallocate them.
</Tip>

## Skills

| Skill | Unlock Requirement | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Better Gutting** | Level 0 | Gives you more entropy from gutting fish |
| **Luck of the Catch** | Level 17 | Gives you more entropy from catching fish |
| **Master Augmenter** | Level 16 | Reduces the cost to craft an augment |
| **Totem Leader** | Level 0 | Reduces your totem cooldown for fish |
| **Divine Judgement** | 15 skill points | Chance to create a tsunami that gives double fish every time you fish inside the circle |
| **Tribal Shout** | 15 skill points | Gives nearby players a percentage of your totem effects (37.5% \+ 5% per 15 additional levels). Totem skills do not overlap. |
| **Combo Catcher** | 15 skill points | Increases your chance for double fish. Max combo: 30 catches, \+5 bonus per 20 levels |
| **Augment Infusion** | 15 skill points | Chance to refund augment crafting materials. 35.5% refund chance with \+3% per 8 levels |
