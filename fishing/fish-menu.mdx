The `/fish menu` command opens the main fishing hub — your central access point for every fishing feature on the server.

![Fish Menu](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FVuGg8o66I8wJ1jDcPiW5%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

## Menu Icons

| Icon | What It Does | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your head / avatar | Fish Stats: view your progress and stats | `/fish stats` |
| Chest | Fishing Deliveries: earn deliveries by fishing | `/fish deliveries` |
| Glistering Melon | Fish Gutting: gut fish for entropy (RNG-based) | `/fish gut` |
| Anvil | Weighing Station: weigh fish to gamble a higher sell price | `/fish scales` |
| Empty Map | Fishing Codex: stores all fish types and caught amounts | `/fish codex` |
| Spyglass | Fishing Skill Tree: manage upgrades and skill points | `/fish skills` |
| Amethyst Shard | Augments: manage your rod's augments and their effects | `/fish augments` / `/fish augment` |
| Totem of Undying | Fishing Totem: unlock more perks starting at fishing level 20 | See [Fishing Totem](/fishing/fishing-totem) |
| Bucket of Tropical Fish | Fishing Market: sell fish you don't want to save for deliveries | `/fish shop` |
