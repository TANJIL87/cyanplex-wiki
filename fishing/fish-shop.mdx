Use the `/fish shop` command to sell your fish for in-game money. Simply add your fish to the menu that opens, then close it. Your chat will tell you how much money you made.

![Fish Shop](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FXRqSjt9JIMwkmPtLCVsD%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

## Featured Fish

The top of the Fish Shop menu shows a **featured fish** — the fish currently on sale. Selling the featured fish earns you **even more money** than usual. The featured fish rotates to a different type every **30 minutes**, so check back often!

## Bonus Money Tip

<Tip>
  Add the **Solar Rage** augment to your fishing rod and hold your rod while you sell your fish for an extra money payout!
</Tip>
