The Fishing Totem is a buildable structure that gives you passive fishing boosts while you stand inside its green particle circle. You can unlock it once you reach **fishing level 20**.

![Fishing Totem](https://minebloom.gitbook.io/play/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3178475287-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FehiTq9ExxDNOpJlm4YQR%252Fuploads%252FhnOpBD7xESOrCczFMkiz%252F.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100)

<Warning>
  You can only have **1 totem per player** — do not lose track of where yours is!
</Warning>

Your fishing rod can cast outside the green circle as long as **you are standing inside it**.

## Materials Required

- 1x Daylight Sensor
- 4x Spruce Stairs
- 2x Observers
- 1x New Anvil (cannot be damaged)
- 1x Green Glazed Terracotta
- 1x Orange Glazed Terracotta

## How to Activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the totem">
    Build the totem exactly as shown in the image above. The structure must be exact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate with your fishing rod">
    Once built, hold your fishing rod and right-click the **bottom Observer** to activate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First-time activation items">
    The **first time** you activate the totem, you'll need to provide the following items:

    - 6x Platinum Fish
    - 16x Crab Scales
    - 16x Crab Claws
    - 16x Dolphin Tails
    - 16x Squid Tentacles
  </Step>
</Steps>
